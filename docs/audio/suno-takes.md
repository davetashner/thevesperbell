# Suno takes

Direct links to the Suno song for every take we use, so nobody has to hunt through the Suno library.
The links open in the owner's Suno account (songs are private unless published).

When a take is picked: move its row from "Awaiting a pick" to "Kept takes" and delete the rejected
alternatives' rows. When a new batch is generated: add a row per take under "Awaiting a pick".
